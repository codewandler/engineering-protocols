# Postmortem: <incident>

## Summary

*What happened, for how long, and who it affected. Readable on its own.*

## Impact

*Quantified: requests, users, data, money, duration.*

## Timeline

*Timestamped events, including what responders believed at each point — the wrong beliefs are the
useful part.*

## Detection

*How it was noticed, how long that took, and whether monitoring or a human found it first.*

## Contributing Factors

*The conditions that made the failure possible. Plural by construction; a single root cause is
usually a stopping point chosen too early.*

## What Worked

*Defences, tooling and decisions that limited the damage — these are worth protecting.*

## Recovery

*What restored service, and whether it was the planned path.*

## Actions

*Each follow-up as a tracked item with an owner, distinguishing what prevents recurrence from what
reduces impact next time.*

## Lessons

*What the team now knows that it did not before, stated so a different team could use it.*
